## The thesis — the binding constraint has moved

At founding (April) the constraint was *existence*. By June it was *reliability* —
and that war was won: zero weekday misses since 2026-07-08, watchdog armed and
proven, health alerts caught the one mid-run death within 24h. The remaining
14 missed days are all weekend/holiday (machine off), and every programmatic
consumer runs on weekdays.

**The binding constraint now is signal quality and noise discipline:**

1. The headline band has been **orange 107 of 118 live days and red the other 11 —
   zero green/yellow — through a +16% SPX rally.** Mechanism: `red>=1 → orange`
   headline rule × two Brief 19 thresholds (crack_spread_321, copper_gold_ratio)
   that have been pinned red 66–80% of live days. Under current thresholds the
   headline would have been orange-or-worse 55% of ALL days 2018–2026 (63% of calm
   2021). The scale is broken, not the market.
2. **0 of 29 threshold sets were ever derived from data, and none has ever been
   revised.** The one empirical recalibration in project history was silently
   reverted within hours (by a commit claiming the opposite).
3. **Alert traffic is majority plumbing** (24 staleness + 7 health + daily
   never-expiring heartbeat vs 22 market alerts in 132 days), and the 22 market
   alerts preceded a ≥3% drawdown *less* often than the base rate. The user-fatigue
   endpoint is already documented (13 ignored watchdog days in July).
4. 5.4% of the composite is a hardcoded constant (manual indicators untouched
   since April 23), and the trading-bot band gate has been dead code since 08-31
   (schedule mismatch neutralizes every read).

The rebuild is therefore **a calibration and noise-discipline rebuild**, not an
infrastructure one. No new indicators, no new alert types, no platform migration.

## REBUILD — the calibration and noise-discipline layer

- **B1 — Threshold recalibration with firing rates** (M–L). New
  `python -m src.threshold_report`: for every indicator, the historical firing
  rate of each band under current thresholds (point-in-time backtest raws,
  2018–2026 + live window), and proposed thresholds hitting target base rates
  (defaults: red ≤5% of days, orange ≤15%, per-indicator judgment overrides
  documented inline). Output = a report artifact + a proposed thresholds.yaml
  diff. **Rates get written as comments next to every threshold** (methodology
  rule 1). This is Brief 19's promised-and-never-run re-check, generalized to
  all 29.
- **B2 — Headline band redesign** (M, design-first, D1). Recommendation (option
  A): headline band = the *score band* of the composite, escalated at most one
  level by breadth (red indicators in ≥2 distinct buckets → +1 level), with
  one-day hysteresis. Kills the `red>=1 → orange` single-indicator veto. Option
  B (composite_short-primary headline) documented and rejected-by-default —
  see Rejected list.
- **B3 — Alert routing rebuild** (M). Two lanes: **market** (band changes, new
  reds under recalibrated thresholds — Pushover push, target <2/month in calm
  regimes) and **plumbing** (staleness, health-recovery notices, feed health —
  dashboard card + Monday digest only; push ONLY for the two critical cases:
  dashboard-stale and publish-failure). Heartbeat expires after its 31 days
  (R2) and does not return.
- **B4 — Regime-weights review (Brief 26) dissolved into an event trigger**
  (S). The calendar-date review has been overdue since 06-20 because its

## Rejected on principle — do not re-litigate

1. **Auto-recalibration that mutates weights/thresholds silently** — already
   locked, stays locked. Recalibration produces *proposals* + a one-word apply.
2. **New indicators or buckets** until B1–B3 ship. The project added 3
   indicators while 0 thresholds got calibrated; breadth of inputs is not the
   constraint. (This includes "consumer energy burden" and any sentiment
   additions.)
3. **New alert types.** Twelve exist; the problem is routing and precision,
   not coverage.
4. **GitHub Actions publish migration, now.** Reliability is no longer
   binding; all 14 misses are weekend/holiday and all programmatic consumers
   are weekday-daytime. Revisit ONLY on the first weekday miss — the spike
   plan in TODO Phase E remains valid then.
5. **Composite_short as the headline (B2 option B).** It fixes the structural-
   level bias but silently redefines the published number two consumers read;
   a 3-year percentile window also makes 2022-style regimes look permanently
   normal. The long window stays primary; the short line stays displayed.
6. **Backtest engine rewrite** to perfectly match live scoring. The divergences
   (no CNN, no regime weights, exclude-vs-dilute on missing data) get *labeled
   honestly* on the card instead (R13). A rewrite is weeks of work to improve
   a number nobody should over-trust anyway.
7. **Paywalled news, portfolio integration** — unchanged locked/blocked status.
8. **Dashboard HTML/layout overhaul** — it serves its users; not the
   constraint.

## Guard rails — gravitational pulls this project's history proves

- **Pull: add another indicator/card/alert instead of calibrating what exists.**
  Rate-limit: any PR adding an indicator must carry its threshold firing rates.
- **Pull: silent fallback accretion.** ~35 swallow sites; a broken feed renders
  as a green dot. New rule: a fallback must either surface in the DATA QUALITY
  card or it doesn't merge.
- **Pull: flattering commit messages.** "Step 0 — restore recalibrated config"
  reverted the recalibration. Commit messages state what the diff does.
- **Pull: promising a follow-up without scheduling it.** Brief 19's "re-check
  in 2 weeks" (never ran), Brief 26's review (2.5 months overdue),
  weights.yaml's "2026-05-30" comment. Every future review gets a default
  action and a date, or it isn't written.
