## REBUILD — Premarket Briefing v1

MICRO's new job: **the 6:30 ET human window into the systems that do have edge.** The bot decides for itself but nothing tells Ian what state the machine is in before the day starts (today's kill-switch discovery is the proof). Read-only, files-on-disk, no cross-project imports — all contracts already exist.

One Pushover, ~8 lines, four sections:

1. **Regime** — from `market_dashboard/data/latest.json`: composite + band, regime, shock type. Staleness check (>36h → flagged STALE).
2. **Bot health** — from `tactical_markets_trading/data/last_successful_run.json` + `account_state.json`: last-success freshness, `entry_ok` + gate reason, open position count, equity vs peak (drawdown %). Today it would have read: *"⚠ bot: entries blocked (kill_switch 5 consecutive losses), last success 8/28, equity $102.7k (−0.0% off peak)."*
3. **Positions & flip proximity** — from `strategy_state_*.json` + live quotes: TQQQ distance to its trailing stop (off `position_peak_price`), SPY distance to 200d/50d MA (router flip levels). Lines within ~2% of triggering get a ⚠ prefix; calm lines stay terse.
4. **Overnight tape** — SPY/QQQ premarket vs prior close (flag |gap| ≥ 1%), VIX level + 5d change.

Mechanics: `run_tactical.py` stays the entrypoint (scheduler untouched); `src/briefing.py` replaces `sector_rotation.py` in the call path; every briefing logged to `data/briefings.jsonl` with `signal_type: "premarket_briefing"`. ~150–200 lines. 3–4 working days.

**Then a two-week read**, same trick that worked in week 1:
- (a) "I acted on a ⚠ line" → tune thresholds, consider backlog items.
- (b) "All noise" → alert-only mode (push only when something is abnormal).
- (c) "Never read it" → delivery/format wrong, not content.

## Backlog — new ideas, pick after the read

1. **Trade postmortems:** a close in bot `trades.jsonl` since yesterday → one line with realized P&L + hold time. This makes the briefing the label pipeline toward the ≥30-trade ML gate.
2. **Event awareness:** hardcoded FOMC/CPI/NFP dates (same pattern as `NYSE_HOLIDAYS`) → "FOMC decision today" line. The zero-API version of the roadmap's economic calendar.
3. **VIX term structure:** ^VIX vs ^VIX3M ratio, flag backwardation flips — genuine 24–48h information, complements MACRO's level-based VIX score.
4. **Priority escalation:** high-priority Pushover only for acute states (stop within 1%, kill switch newly tripped); routine briefing stays normal priority.
5. **Weekly digest (Sunday PM):** week's equity vs SPY, regime-day counts, gate trips.
6. **Sector dispersion line:** widest 5d spread as context, no direction claimed. Honest recommendation: skip — the question is answered.

**Rejected on principle:** reversion flip (no stable edge), adaptive switch (parameter luck, doubles parameter surface), ML confidence (gate unmet), HTML output (Pushover-only stands), intraday polling (this is a 6:30 product).
